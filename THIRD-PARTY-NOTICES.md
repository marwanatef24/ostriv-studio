Ostriv Studio is an independent community project. Ostriv and its assets belong to their respective owners. The original Ostriv Resource Editor was created by Vinay Choudhary; its [published feature description](https://vinay-at-work.blogspot.com/2021/03/all-i-have-made-new-resource-editor.html) and local reverse-engineering observations informed compatibility research. Resource identifiers and building-class mappings were transcribed from the editor and checked where possible against the game; they are not a claim that every entry has been verified on every game build.
